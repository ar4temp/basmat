About Us

A Trusted Partner in Human Capital

Founded in Riyadh, Basmat Al Mawared is a Saudi human resources company built on a single principle: the right people, deployed the right way. We combine deep local market knowledge with rigorous governance to help organizations build capable, compliant, and future-ready teams.

Our name reflects our purpose — to leave a lasting imprint on the organizations we serve and the national talent we help develop.
